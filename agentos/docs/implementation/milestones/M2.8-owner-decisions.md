## Decision contract

OD-01 through OD-36 are owner-approved decisions for the current M2.8 scope. Each entry records the approved conclusion, alternatives considered, rationale, risk, reversibility, and required evidence. Approval closes the planning contract; it does not authorize work outside the listed scope.

## OD-01 — M2.8 target state

- Decision: M2.8 completes Verification and Cutover Readiness; it does not perform Production Cutover.
- Options: readiness and rehearsal only; controlled production cutover; defer the milestone.
- Recommendation: Owner-approved readiness, verification, and cutover planning only.
- Rationale: the M2 milestone must close its acceptance and rollback evidence without prematurely implementing the M3 production transition.
- Risk: treating readiness evidence as cutover authorization would widen scope and create irreversible operational risk.
- Reversibility: high while work remains documentation, verification, and isolated rehearsal.
- Required Evidence: complete M2 acceptance inventory, restore rehearsal, cutover plan, and formal gate.
- Owner Status: APPROVED
